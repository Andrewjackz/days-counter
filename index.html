💾 What is the JSON Backup?

Think of the JSON Backup as a personal photocopier for all your data. When you tap the button, the app saves a complete copy of every night you've ever logged — every date, city, and country — into a small file on your phone or computer.

Why does this matter?
This app stores your data inside your browser — not on the internet, not in a cloud, not on any server. That's great for privacy, but it means if you clear your browser, switch phones, or your browser resets, all your data could disappear. The backup file is your insurance policy.
What to do with the file?
Save it somewhere safe — your email, iCloud, Google Drive, or just your Downloads folder. To restore your data on any device, go to Export → "Import JSON Backup" and select the file. All your entries will reappear instantly.
Weekly reminder
The app will remind you once a week to take a backup. It only takes one tap. For tax purposes, your travel history is valuable — treat it like you would a passport.
